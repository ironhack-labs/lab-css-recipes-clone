Apple Pie

This was my grandmother's apple pie recipe. I have never seen another one quite like it. It will always be my favorite and has won me several first place prizes in local competitions.

Ingredients

  • 1 recipe pastry for a 9 inch double crust pie
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
  • 8 Granny Smith apples - peeled, cored and sliced

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Melt the butter in a saucepan.
  3. Stir in flour to form a paste.
  4. Add water, white sugar and brown sugar and bring to a boil.
  5. Reduce temperature and let simmer.
  6. Place the bottom crust in your pan and fill with apples.
  7. Cover with lattice crust and pour mixture over it.
  8. Bake 15 minutes. Reduce temperature and bake 35–45 minutes.
