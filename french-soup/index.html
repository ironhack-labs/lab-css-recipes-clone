French Onion Soup

French Onion Soup We have been trying French onion soup in restaurants for years and my family and friends agree none can compare to my recipe for taste and simplicity of preparation.

Ingredients


  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cups sliced onions
  • 4 cans (10.5 ounces each) beef broth
  • 2 tablespoons dry sherry (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 4 slices French bread
  • 4 slices provolone cheese
  • 2 slices Swiss cheese, chopped
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Directions


  1. Melt the butter with the olive oil in a large 8-quart pot over medium heat.
  2. Add the onions and stir continuously until they are tender and translucent. Do not let them brown.
  3. Add the beef broth, sherry, and thyme. Season with salt and pepper, and simmer for 30 minutes.
  4. Top each slice of bread with a slice of provolone, 1/2 slice of chopped Swiss, and 1 tablespoon of Parmesan cheese.
  5. Place the bowls on a baking sheet and broil in the preheated oven until the cheese bubbles and is lightly browned.
